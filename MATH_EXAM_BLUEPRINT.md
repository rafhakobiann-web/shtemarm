# Shtemarm Mathematics Exam Blueprint

## Exam mode
- Exactly 65 questions per complete exam attempt.
- Questions are selected randomly from the current mathematics practice bank.
- The topic counts remain fixed on every attempt; only the selected questions vary.
- Results include the correct answer and explanation for every selected question.

## Important content status
The current practice bank contains 73 original educational questions aligned to broad topics found in the two mathematics books. It is not yet a full transcription of every exercise in either book. The book-by-book answer key must be compiled separately by book, chapter, section, and exercise number; answers should be verified against the supplied PDFs before being marked complete.
